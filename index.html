About Us

Your trusted security products partner in Dindigul

SB System Service is a wholesale dealer of electronic security and surveillance products based in Dindigul. We are distributors of Hikvision, Prama and HI-FOCUS, and sub-distributors of CP PLUS, Dahua, Trueview, Qubo, Maxxion, Tiandy, Securemax and Fyber. We stock and supply CCTV cameras, DVRs/NVRs, biometric attendance machines, access control systems and all the accessories needed for a complete installation.

We work with CCTV dealers, installers, system integrators, builders and business owners – offering genuine products from popular brands, competitive wholesale pricing and dependable after-sales support.
